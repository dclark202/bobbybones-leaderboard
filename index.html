BobbyBones by version

Each version’s rating against people, oldest to newest; the filled point is the version playing now.

A player is ranked from their first full game; 20 are not ranked yet (# –).

The rating counts games won and lost against BobbyBones, on either server: Glicko-1, one update per game; everyone starts at 1500. A quit counts as a loss if the player quitting is behind after more than 5 minutes; any other quit does not count. A difference of 100 points means the higher one wins about 64% of the games between the two. ± is twice the rating's uncertainty, which is large after only a few games. Frags are shown beside the rating and do not move it.

Names are the in-game name a player was last seen with; !anonymous in the game shows a player as Anonymous here and in the game history. Updated a few minutes after a game. Board as of 2026-10-31 01:05 UTC.
